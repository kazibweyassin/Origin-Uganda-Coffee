01  EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Chick Filler is a Ugandan quick-service chicken brand designed for speed,
value, consistency, and convenience. We propose launching the first Chick
Filler outlet at Rubis Kakoba in Mbarara.

The outlet would serve motorists, boda boda riders, taxi drivers, travellers,
nearby workers, students, and residents who want a hot, affordable meal while
refuelling or passing through Kakoba. The compact kiosk would operate as an
independent Chick Filler business while strengthening the Rubis customer
experience.

THE PROPOSED TRADE

Rubis provides an approved space, access to utilities, and agreed promotion.
Chick Filler funds and manages the kiosk, including equipment, staff, stock,
licensing, cleaning, and day-to-day operations.

LAUNCH OFFER

Buy UGX 200,000 or more in fuel at Rubis Kakoba and receive a voucher for two
pieces of Chick Filler chicken, subject to the agreed terms and daily limit.

02  WHY RUBIS KAKOBA SHOULD PARTNER WITH CHICK FILLER

Benefit to Rubis              Why it matters
More fuel volume              A food reward can encourage larger fill-ups.
Customer loyalty              Hot food gives customers another reason to choose
							  and return to Rubis Kakoba.
Non-fuel income               Rubis can earn fixed rent, revenue share, or both.
More customer footfall        Food customers may also buy fuel, drinks, snacks,
							  car care products, and other station services.
Stronger convenience offer    Fuel and fresh food create a more useful stop.
Low operating burden          Chick Filler funds, staffs, cleans, and manages
							  the outlet.
Shared visibility             Chick Filler marketing will feature Rubis Kakoba
							  as the launch location.

THE PITCH IN ONE LINE

Chick Filler gives Rubis Kakoba a new food and income stream without requiring
Rubis to build, staff, or operate a restaurant.

03  PARTNERSHIP PROPOSAL

We request a small, approved kiosk or container space at Rubis Kakoba, subject
to Rubis site, safety, traffic-flow, and brand requirements.

Option   Structure                                      Benefit to Rubis
A        Fixed rent: UGX 1.5M-2.5M per month            Predictable income
B        Revenue share: 10%-15% of gross food sales     Upside as sales grow
C        Hybrid: UGX 800K-1.2M per month plus 5%        Base income plus upside
D        Three-month pilot, then agree long-term terms  Measured launch risk

RECOMMENDED START

Begin with a three-month pilot under Option C, subject to site assessment and
final commercial negotiation. After the pilot, both parties review sales,
customer response, operating performance, and the promotion before agreeing a
longer-term three- to five-year agreement.

* Non-transferable
* While stocks last

05  MARKET AND LOCATION

WHY KAKOBA, MBARARA

Rubis Kakoba can serve customers moving through a busy urban and regional
commercial area. Its forecourt gives Chick Filler visibility, security, and a
steady flow of potential customers without requiring a separate high-street
restaurant site.

TARGET CUSTOMERS

* Motorists refuelling at Rubis Kakoba
* Boda boda riders and taxi drivers
* Travellers passing through Mbarara
* Nearby office, retail, and service workers
* Students and local residents
* Families and groups seeking a quick meal

LOCAL COMPETITION

* Existing chicken and chips outlets
* Roadside food vendors
* Supermarkets and convenience shops
* National and regional quick-service restaurants

CHICK FILLER ADVANTAGE

* A visible location inside a trusted fuel station
* Fast service and affordable portions
* Consistent preparation and food-safety procedures
* Fuel-and-food promotions unique to the Rubis partnership
* A menu suited to takeaway, dine-in-at-the-station, and delivery

06  OPERATIONS PLAN

PROPOSED SPACE

Approximately 15-30 square metres for a kiosk or container, subject to Rubis
approval, traffic-flow requirements, power and water availability, ventilation,
fire safety, waste handling, and local authority approval.

INITIAL EQUIPMENT

* Commercial pressure fryer or approved commercial fryer
* Hot holding and display cabinet
* Refrigeration and food storage
* Breading and preparation table
* Hand-washing and cleaning equipment
* POS system, receipt printer, and CCTV where appropriate

The final equipment list and budget will be confirmed after a site survey and
supplier quotations. The earlier estimate of USD 55,650 is a planning figure,
not a final quotation.

STAFF AND HOURS

* Four to six staff at launch: cook, cashier, server, cleaner, and supervisor
* Proposed hours: 7:00am-10:00pm, seven days a week
* Opening hours to follow Rubis site rules, customer demand, and security needs

COMPLIANCE

Chick Filler will obtain and maintain the approvals required for the site,
including Mbarara City health and trading approvals, URA tax registration,
food-safety requirements, waste management, fire and occupational-safety
requirements, and any applicable UNBS or NEMA requirements.

10  RISKS AND MITIGATION

Risk                         Mitigation
Food safety                  Hygiene controls, training, temperature records,
							 approved suppliers, and regular checks.
Promotion abuse              Numbered vouchers, transaction limits, same-day
							 redemption, and daily reconciliation.
Cash leakage or theft        POS controls, stock counts, CCTV where approved,
							 and separation of duties.
Slow initial sales           Pilot period, menu testing, combos, delivery,
							 corporate orders, and local marketing.
Traffic or safety conflict   Rubis-approved layout, signage, access, waste,
							 fire, and operating procedures.
Commercial disagreement      Written agreement covering rent, revenue share,
							 utilities, branding, maintenance, and exit terms.
